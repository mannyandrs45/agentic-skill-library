# Hotel Selector — Scoring Rubric

This reference defines the 1–5 scoring criteria for each qualitative dimension in Step 1b.

## Hotel Aesthetic (Entrance, Lobby, Concierge)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5 | Striking, design-forward space. Clear visual identity. Lobby feels like a destination in itself. Concierge area is polished and inviting. |
| 4 | Well-designed, cohesive aesthetic. Above average effort in decor and atmosphere. Minor inconsistencies. |
| 3 | Clean and functional but unremarkable. Standard hotel lobby. No strong design identity. |
| 2 | Dated or mismatched decor. Feels neglected or generic. Lobby is purely transactional. |
| 1 | Unappealing, poorly maintained, or visually confusing. Negative first impression. |

## Wow Factors (Amenities: Spas, Views, Restaurants & Bars)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5 | Multiple standout amenities that are attractions in their own right (e.g., Michelin-level restaurant, world-class spa, iconic rooftop bar). Guests specifically mention these as highlights. |
| 4 | At least one truly impressive amenity plus solid supporting options. Amenities add significant value to the stay. |
| 3 | Amenities are present and functional but not remarkable. Standard pool, basic gym, average restaurant. |
| 2 | Limited amenities. What exists feels like an afterthought. |
| 1 | No meaningful amenities beyond the room itself, or amenities are in poor condition. |

## Room Layout & Views

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5 | Spacious, thoughtfully designed room. Excellent storage, quality bathroom, premium bedding. View is a genuine feature (landmark, panoramic city, ocean). Layout accommodates the travel party perfectly. |
| 4 | Above average room. Good layout with minor compromises. View is pleasant. Comfortable for the party. |
| 3 | Standard hotel room. Functional but not memorable. View is unremarkable (courtyard, street). Adequate for party size. |
| 2 | Below average room. Cramped, poor layout, or disappointing view relative to price. May feel tight for the party. |
| 1 | Room is a significant negative. Too small, poorly maintained, or misrepresented. View is actively unpleasant (construction, wall, dumpster). |

## Photo Authenticity

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5 | Traveler photos match or exceed official photos. The hotel looks as good (or better) in reality. High confidence in what you see is what you get. |
| 4 | Traveler photos are consistent with official photos. Minor differences in lighting or angle. Generally trustworthy. |
| 3 | Noticeable differences between official and traveler photos, but nothing deal-breaking. Some cherry-picking in official photos. |
| 2 | Significant discrepancies. Official photos use heavy editing, misleading angles, or show renovated rooms that aren't standard. Traveler photos reveal a noticeably different experience. |
| 1 | Misleading official photos. Reality is substantially worse. This is a red flag for the hotel's integrity. |

## Location Micro-Score

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5 | Perfectly positioned for the user's priorities. Walking distance (<10 min) to top restaurants, attractions, and nightlife. Safe, vibrant neighborhood. Excellent transit access. |
| 4 | Very well located. Most key points of interest within 15 min walk or short transit ride. Good neighborhood feel. |
| 3 | Acceptable location. Some key interests are walkable, others require transit. Neighborhood is fine but not a highlight. |
| 2 | Inconvenient for the user's priorities. Most interests require transit or driving. Neighborhood is lackluster or slightly off. |
| 1 | Poor location relative to stated interests. Isolated, hard to reach, or in an area that conflicts with user preferences (e.g., user wants nightlife but hotel is in quiet suburbs). |

## Personalized Weight Assignment

Map the user's ranked priorities (from Step 0) to dimensions:

| User Priority | Maps To Dimension(s) |
|---|---|
| Walkability to restaurants & nightlife | Location Micro-Score |
| Pool or rooftop | Wow Factors |
| Spa & wellness | Wow Factors |
| On-site restaurant/bar quality | Wow Factors |
| Room size & layout | Room Layout & Views |
| Views (city, ocean, landmark) | Room Layout & Views |
| Quiet & privacy | Room Layout & Views, Location Micro-Score |
| Kid-friendly amenities | Wow Factors, Room Layout & Views |
| Iconic/Instagram-worthy aesthetic | Hotel Aesthetic, Wow Factors |

When a priority maps to multiple dimensions, split the weight multiplier across them (e.g., a 3x weight becomes 1.5x on each).
